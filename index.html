The brand

Why VIA exists

We don’t make sneakers. We make places you can wear.

01

Limited editions with a place attached

Every pair belongs to a specific city. Produced in small runs, never restocked. You don’t just buy shoes — you get a story you can tell and a design that carries the character of a place.

Unique · Limited · Tied to a city
02

Design that feels like the city

Berlin is muted, industrial, precise. Amalfi is sun-faded, open, coastal. Materials, silhouette and colour are chosen so each edition instantly evokes its place — no two pairs look or feel the same.

Country-specific materials & mood
03

20% of every purchase returns to the place

When you buy a pair dedicated to a city, 20% of the amount goes directly to an environmental organisation working in that region. Berlin supports greening and the protection of Tempelhofer Feld. Amalfi supports Mediterranean sea and coastal protection.

Buy a pair → leave a positive footprint

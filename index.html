Questions

FAQ

Short answers. Open only what you need.

VIA is a student brand concept. The site, survey and visual language explore how a city-based sneaker brand could look and feel. Not a live store yet.

In the concept, 20% of each purchase would support an environmental project tied to that city — e.g. greening around Tempelhofer Feld in Berlin, and Mediterranean coastal protection for Amalfi.

Berlin 01 and Amalfi 05 are the current editions in the concept. Tokyo 02 and Paris 03 are marked as coming next on the drop calendar.

Each pair is meant to feel like a chapter of a place — small runs, no restocks. Scarcity supports the idea of a story you can own and talk about, not a mass product.
